# Flex Companion v0.6.0

- Adds four persistent meter layouts: Simple Analogue, Digital Select, Multi Analogue and Digital Multimeter.
- Adds a cached-face compound analogue meter with three colour-coded, independently selectable TX needles.
- Adds a compact digital multimeter showing RX plus Power, SWR, Processor, Mic, Vdd, Current and PA Temperature together.
- Keeps meter presentation source-paced and coalesced. Extra FLEX meter subscriptions are enabled only for the values required by the selected layout.
- Migrates existing Analogue/Digital preferences automatically.
