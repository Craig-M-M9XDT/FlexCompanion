# Flex Companion v0.6.1

- Widens the simple and multi analogue meters to make better use of the radio panel.
- Uses a restrained elliptical broadcast-meter scale rather than leaving unused space around a small circular dial.
- Keeps the meters responsive when the panel narrows in dual-radio mode.
- Adds immediate-attack peak markers to the simple analogue meter and all three multi-analogue tracks.
- Holds each peak for 0.85 seconds, then releases it smoothly toward the live reading with a 0.65-second decay constant.
- Preserves cached static meter faces and source-paced FLEX meter updates.
